Use videos as the beginning of your research

Our video collection introduces refugee experiences, forced displacement and humanitarian responses in a format that students can discuss. Before sharing a video, check who produced it, when it was published and whether its facts link to an authoritative source. A personal story can help us understand one person's experience, but it should never be treated as the experience of every refugee.

Questions to ask while watching

  • Does the video distinguish refugees, asylum seekers, migrants and internally displaced people?
  • Are statistics connected to a source and reporting year?
  • Does the person telling their story appear to have given informed permission?
  • Does the video show people with dignity instead of using frightening images for attention?

Build your understanding

Start with What is a refugee?, continue with our guide to commonly confused displacement terms, and read the website's source and editorial policy. For classroom resources, visit UNHCR's teaching materials.
