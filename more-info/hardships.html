Hardships of Refugees

Understand the challenges refugees face before, during and after seeking safety, and learn how people can help.

Refugees often face many challenges before leaving home, during their journey and after arriving in a new country. Although these hardships can be difficult, people around the world work together to help refugees rebuild their lives.

🏠 PHASE 1: AT HOME

Many refugees are forced to leave because their homes are no longer safe.

  • War
    Fighting can destroy homes, schools and hospitals, making it dangerous for families to stay.

  • Persecution
    Some people are treated unfairly because of their race, religion, nationality or beliefs.

  • Human Rights Violations
    People may experience violence, lose important freedoms or be treated unfairly.

  • Climate Disasters
    Floods, droughts, storms and other natural disasters can force families to leave their homes.

🚶 PHASE 2: DURING THE JOURNEY

The journey to safety can be long, exhausting and dangerous.

  • Hunger
    Food can be difficult to find during long journeys.

  • Thirst
    Clean drinking water is not always available.

  • Fear and Uncertainty
    Many refugees do not know where they will sleep or when they will finally be safe.

  • Long Journeys
    Some families travel for weeks or even months before reaching a safe place.

🏡 PHASE 3: AFTER ARRIVAL

Reaching a safe country is an important step, but refugees still face many challenges as they begin a new life.

  • Language Barriers
    Learning a new language can make it difficult to communicate, attend school and find work.

  • Finding Acceptance
    Some refugees may experience discrimination or bullying before they are accepted by their new community.

  • Homesickness
    Many refugees miss their family, friends and the place they once called home.

  • Starting Again
    Finding housing, education and employment often takes time and patience.

🌍 Solutions

Although refugees face many hardships, people around the world can help them build a brighter future.

  • 🏠 Provide safe homes and temporary shelters.

  • 🍎 Donate food, clean water, clothing and blankets.

  • 📚 Help refugee children continue their education.

  • 🤝 Welcome refugees with kindness and respect.

  • 💙 Volunteer or donate to organisations that support refugees.

  • 🌎 Countries can work together to protect people fleeing danger.

📰 Stories of Kindness

Around the world, many people have shown kindness to refugees. Here are two inspiring examples.

🇫🇮 Finland's Prime Minister Welcomes Refugees

Finland's Prime Minister, Juha Sipilä, offered his own home to help refugees and encouraged others to think about how they could help people escaping danger. His actions showed that kindness and leadership can inspire others to support those in need.

🇭🇺 Hungarians Donate Shoes to Refugees

Many refugees arrived with very few belongings, and some did not even have shoes. Hundreds of volunteers donated shoes, blankets, baby clothes and food to help them. Their generosity showed that even small acts of kindness can make a big difference.

💙 Real-Life Example

When Russia invaded Ukraine in 2022, millions of people were forced to leave their homes. Families travelled to neighbouring countries such as Poland, Romania and Slovakia to find safety. Many experienced the same hardships described on this page before beginning to rebuild their lives.

❤️ Every Small Act Matters

Helping refugees doesn't always require doing something big. Donating food, clothing or school supplies, volunteering your time, welcoming newcomers or simply learning about refugee experiences can all make a positive difference.

Every act of kindness gives hope to someone who has had to leave everything behind. By working together, we can help refugees feel safe, respected and welcome in their new communities.

Together, we can help refugees find safety, hope and a brighter future.
