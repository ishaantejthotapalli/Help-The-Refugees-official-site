Hardships of Refugees

Understand the challenges refugees face before, during and after seeking safety.

There are 3 phases of problems that challenge refugees in their daily lives.

This is where the main problem is because it is the reason they even seek refuge in another country, the at home problem!


What they usually face:


1) War: This is the most common reason refugees leave because their country is being attacked.


2) Persecution: Harm for unfair reasons like race or religion.


3) Human Rights Violations: Government influence, torture etc. Could be a reason why people migrate.


4) Climate Disaster: A natural disaster or consistent natural disasters can make people migrate.

The journey is very troublesome. Here are 3 things people face.


1) Hunger: People will get hungry because the journey is very long.


2) Thirst: People will get thirsty easily and have a risk of death.


3) Hope: People may lose hope of ever reaching safety during the journey.

Here are the most annoying things people face at the destination.


1) Language Barrier: People might miss out on opportunities due to not knowing the language and will take time to learn.


2) No Acceptance: People might not be liked by the new community and might be troubled or bullied by them.


3) Homesickness and Loneliness: People might miss home and family and be lonely and sad.


4) No Confidence: People will be tired and find it hard to start over.

When Russia invaded Ukraine, millions of people left their homes and sought refuge in multiple countries. Many of them experienced the same hardships shown above before finding safety.
