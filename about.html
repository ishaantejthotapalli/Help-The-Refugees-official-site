About Help The Refugees

Help The Refugees is a student-created educational website that helps children learn about refugees and forced displacement through clear explanations, interactive games, maps and current data.

Created by Ishaan and Bramhha · Last reviewed 26 July 2026

Our mission

Our aim is to make a difficult global subject understandable without turning anyone's experience into entertainment. We use compassionate language, explain uncertainty, and remind readers that every statistic represents a person with a unique story.

How we choose and review information

Educational explanations are written in our own words and checked against primary humanitarian sources. We prefer information published by the United Nations and established international agencies. When a figure has a reporting year, we display that year instead of presenting it as a real-time count.

  • Definitions and global displacement: checked against the UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR).
  • Children's rights and education: checked against UNICEF.
  • Internal displacement: checked against the Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre.

Statistics methodology

The homepage data workflow checks public UNHCR Refugee Data Finder datasets. The displayed displacement total combines the latest usable UNHCR population figures with relevant UNRWA and IDMC values when those datasets are available. The child percentage is calculated from reported age bands. Because countries report at different times, figures may describe different reporting periods and may later be revised.

The website shows the source reporting year and the date on which the data was checked. An automated check does not mean the source publishes a new figure every hour. If validation fails, the website keeps the last known good data instead of displaying an unverified number.

Editorial commitments

  • Use child-friendly, accurate and non-frightening language.
  • Avoid stereotypes and never associate nationality, culture or appearance with danger.
  • Distinguish refugees, asylum seekers, migrants and internally displaced people.
  • Link important factual claims to authoritative sources.
  • Correct meaningful errors and update the review date when content changes.
  • Clearly label simplified or illustrative activities, maps and games.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice wording that is unclear, a broken source, or information that may be outdated, please use our feedback page. We review corrections against primary sources before updating the site.
