Welcome

Millions of people around the world have been forced to leave their homes because of conflict, war, persecution and natural disasters. This website aims to educate visitors about refugee experiences through interactive games, informative resources and real-world crisis information.

Explore, learn and discover how every person can help make a difference.

Why Refugees Need Help

🏠 Forced to Leave Home

Refugees are forced to leave their homes because of war, conflict, persecution or natural disasters. Many have to leave everything behind with very little notice.

🍞 Basic Needs

Food, clean water, shelter, healthcare and education are essential. Humanitarian organisations work hard to provide these necessities to displaced families.

❤️ Hope for the Future

Every act of kindness makes a difference. Raising awareness helps people understand refugee experiences and inspires communities to support those in need.
